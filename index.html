How to play

Move, hold to fire, and time two abilities. Entropy telegraphs every heavy attack on the floor before it lands, so the whole fight is a reading exercise.

Controls

WASDMove — that's the whole movement layer
MouseAim — the gun fires by itself and the aim snaps to what you point near
SpaceDash — damages what you pass through, and nothing can touch you mid-dash
SpaceTime-Swap — with a decoy alive the same button trades places with it, and the dash steps aside
EEcho — a decoy of your last few seconds, fighting for you
TSwitch between night and daylight
EscPause
  • One button does both movement abilities. No decoy on the field means dash; a decoy on the field means swap. You never have to choose under pressure.
  • Dash through a crowd rather than away from it. The invulnerable window outlasts the contact, and the dash itself hurts.
  • Enemies chase whatever is nearest, so an echo dropped on one side splits the room in half.
  • Wardens hook you and shrink the room. Kill the Warden or accept the leash — the tether ring is drawn on the floor.
  • Revenants arrive where you were heading. Change direction as they blink, not after.
  • Hexers rotate three beams through the whole chamber. Pick the gap early and walk with it, not against it.
  • Howitzer brackets on the floor are shells already in the air. The ground they land on keeps burning.
  • Gold-crowned enemies are elites: much tougher, and one of them will be armoured, frenzied or rigged to detonate.
  • Kills close together fill the gold line. When it fills, surge fires on its own — you never have to watch for it.
